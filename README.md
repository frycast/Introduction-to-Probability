# Introduction to Probability
Some detailed notes following 30 lectures at La Trobe University, Melbourne, introducing probability theory and calculus at the undergraduate first / second year level. Special thanks to Associate Professor Katherine Seaton who designed and taught the course on which these notes are based. All words written are my own, though the notes cover the same topics, in roughly the same order, as the original course.

In 2017, the course coordinator wrote:
> "At over 300 pages it is a fantastic resource. The text is aimed at helping students in the subject and this year I have made it available \[to students\]. It has been very well received by students, and is my first recommendation to students wanting some further reading beyond the basic materials I provide."
