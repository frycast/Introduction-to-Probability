\section{Appendix} \label{appendix B}
We will prove that
\[
\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{M}{k}\binom{N-M}{n-k} = \binom{N}{n} \tag{$\star$}
\]
Consider the expression
\[
(1+x)^M(1+x)^n = (1+x)^{M+n}.
\]
Using the binomial theorem to expand the brackets on the left hand side we get,
\[
\squac{\sum_{k=0}^M \binom{M}{k}x^k}\squac{\sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k}x^k}
\]
If we expand the brackets on the left hand side we will find that the coefficient of $x^n$ is
\[
\sum_{k=0}^M\binom{M}{k}\binom{N-M}{n-k}
\]
