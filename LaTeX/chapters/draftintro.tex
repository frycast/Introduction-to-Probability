For most readers, the initial reaction to these notes will be one of turmoil. The book appears \textit{massive}. It certainly is larger than any of the course notes used in any of the undergraduate subjects in the math department at La Trobe, at the time of writing. We will be covering basically all of the material in this book, during the next 12 weeks. If this is causing you to panic then please bear the following in mind: These notes did not exist prior to 2016; there used to be \textit{no subject text}. Every page in this book is designed to make it easier for you to understand the concepts in this course. Why is the book larger than other subject texts? It is because the author is obsessed with making everything exceptionally clear. \\

If you are already a math student then much of the information in this book can be skimmed over. The target audience is students with very little math background.
